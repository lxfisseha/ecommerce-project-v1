Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

You will receive an order confirmation message via SMS or Telegram immediately after placing your order. You can use the Order ID provided in that message to check the status.

What is the delivery time?

Most orders are delivered within 2-3 business days within Addis Ababa.

How can I cancel my order?

Please contact the merchant directly using the contact information provided on the product detail page before the order is marked as 'shipped'.
